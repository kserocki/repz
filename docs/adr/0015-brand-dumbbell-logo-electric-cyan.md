# ADR-0015: Brand: dumbbell logo, electric cyan

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-10-28)

## Context
The violet brand colour was rejected. The logo was designed from a reference sketch.

## Decision
Logo = 7-part dumbbell + monoline wordmark (`brand/LOGO.md`), generated by scripts and used 1:1. Brand colour `#00C2E0` seeds the M3 scheme (Fidelity). Text on cyan is always ink.

## Consequences
Design v0.2 is regenerated from this seed. The logo is animated from its parts (MOT-01, 25–29).
